Manual sliders use each joint’s calibrated MIN/CENTER/MAX only; uncalibrated joints cannot be commanded in Manual mode. Leader Control uses independent Leader/Follower LeRobot ranges. With Leader Control armed at torque ON, the Follower holds its present pose, waits for fresh Leader measurements, then aligns at bounded speed before following.
